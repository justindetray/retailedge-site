Methodology & Signal Construction
RetailEdge v2.3 · WSC Internal Research · Not for distribution
📡 Retail Crowding Score (Weight: 0.30)

Measures the degree to which retail investors (identified via Vanda Research net buy data and Robinhood popularity rankings) are crowded into a position relative to their trailing 252-day history. Computed as the percentile rank of 5-day net retail buy flow divided by ADV.

Formula: CrowdScore = Pctile(NetRetailBuy₅d / ADV, 252d window)

High score (>70) indicates retail is heavily concentrated in the name — consistent with contrarian short-term mean-reversion opportunity when regime gate is open.

Ref: Barber & Odean (2008) "All That Glitters: The Effect of Attention and News on the Buying Behavior of Individual and Institutional Investors." RFS 21(2). Vanda Research Retail Tracking Methodology, 2024.
📉 Dip-Buy Ratio Score (Weight: 0.25)

Tracks the ratio of retail buy-on-dip volume to total retail buy volume over rolling 5-day windows. A declining ratio signals "dip-buy exhaustion" — retail capitulation that historically precedes mean-reversion recoveries.

Formula: DipBuyZ = (DipRatio₅d − μ₆₃d) / σ₆₃d

The z-score below −1.0 is required to pass the regime gate exhaustion condition. The raw ratio is used as the per-ticker sub-score (0–100 percentile).

Ref: Da, Engelberg & Gao (2011) "In Search of Attention." J. Finance 66(5). Welch (2022) "The Wisdom of the Robinhood Crowd." J. Finance 77(3).
⚡ 0DTE Options Score (Weight: 0.25)

Zero-days-to-expiry put/call ratio captures speculative retail hedging behavior. Elevated put buying from retail (identified via small-lot <10 contract screens from ORATS) at extreme percentiles indicates fear-driven overhedging.

Formula: ZDTEScore = Pctile(SmallLot_PC_Ratio₅d, 252d)

Current aggregate 0DTE score: 76.2 (91st percentile). High 0DTE put buying is a strong contrarian signal when concurrent with VIX spike and GEX negative conditions.

Ref: Manaster & Rendleman (1982); Muravyev, Pearson & Broussard (2013). SpotGamma 0DTE Flow Analysis, 2024. ORATS Data Dictionary v4.1.
🧠 Sentiment Score (Weight: 0.20)

A composite of CNN Fear & Greed Index (40% weight), AAII Bull-Bear spread (30%), NAAIM Exposure Index (20%), and social media sentiment proxy from StockTwits volume/sentiment ratio (10%). Normalized to 0–100 (0 = extreme fear, 100 = extreme greed).

Formula: SentScore = 100 − NormalizedFear (inverted: high = greed = more retail crowding)

Current: 34 (Fear zone) — moderate contrarian signal. Extreme fear readings (<20) historically correlate with best forward returns.

Ref: Baker & Wurgler (2006) "Investor Sentiment and the Cross-Section of Stock Returns." J. Finance 61(4). Brown & Cliff (2004) "Investor Sentiment and the Near-Term Stock Market." J. Empirical Finance 11(1).
🔒 Composite Score & Gate Logic

The composite score is a weighted linear combination of sub-scores: C = 0.30×Crowd + 0.25×DipBuy + 0.25×ZDTE + 0.20×Sent

When the Regime Gate is closed, all composite scores are multiplied by 0.45× to reflect lower confidence in the contrarian signal during non-extreme regimes. Gate opens when ≥3 of 4 primary conditions are simultaneously met.

Alert threshold at 75 triggers a position-level notification to the portfolio management team. Blackout flags suppress signals during earnings windows (±3 days) and FOMC meeting days.

Internal calibration: WSC Research, Jan 2024–Apr 2026 (494 trading days). Signal decay tested against SPX forward returns T+1, T+5, T+20.
⚙️ GEX & Regime Classification

Gamma Exposure (GEX) measures the aggregate dealer gamma position across the options market. Positive GEX (dealers long gamma) dampens realized vol as dealers sell into rallies and buy dips. Negative GEX (dealers short gamma) amplifies moves.

Regime Classification: Uses a Hidden Markov Model with 3 states (Calm, Elevated, Crisis) trained on VIX, VVIX, HYG spread, and SPX realized vol (21-day). Regime confidence reflects HMM state probability for the current state.

Current state: Elevated (64% confidence). Previous: Calm (Feb 2026). Transition trigger: VIX spike to 36.4 on Mar 4, 2026.

Ref: SpotGamma GEX Methodology; Hull (2018) Options, Futures & Other Derivatives Ch. 19. Hamilton (1989) "A New Approach to the Economic Analysis of Nonstationary Time Series." Econometrica 57(2).
